# Worked example: "God left me on seen"

The reel this studio was built around. Question: *I've been praying and I don't hear God. Does that mean he isn't
there?* Audience: young people in India. Metaphor: a chat left on "Seen".

Compositions: `Seen` (80 s original), `Seen1` and `Seen2` (the two-part cut, about 39 s each). Code in
`src/flat/Seen.tsx`.

## Part 1 — "God left me on seen" (39 s)

> God left me on seen.
> I prayed. Nothing. Prayed louder. Nothing. I even tried the serious voice. "O Heavenly Father!" Nothing.
> David. Wrote half your worship songs. He also wrote: "How long, Lord? Will you forget me forever?" That's not a
> deleted scene. God kept it in the Bible.
> Mother Teresa. Kolkata. In her private letters, she said God felt absent. For over forty years. She still showed
> up every morning.
> So the silence doesn't mean you're doing it wrong. You're in very good company.
> But here's the twist. God was replying the whole time. That's part two.
> Which is funny, because I really thought… *(loops to the first line)*

## Part 2 — "God texted me back" (40 s)

> God texted me back. Four times. I missed every single one.
> Last time, I decided God left me on seen. But he'd been talking the whole time. In four ways.
> One: his Word. You're waiting for a voice note. He already sent a whole book.
> Two: people. That friend who called exactly when you were falling apart? That wasn't network luck.
> Three: that sermon. The one where you looked at the pastor like… "Who told you?"
> Four: scroll up. The door that closed. The person who appeared. The thing you survived. There's one thread
> running through all of it. It's him.
> He didn't leave you on seen. You were checking the wrong inbox.
> Because it turns out… *(loops to the first line)*

## How it got there

1. **First draft, 6.5/10.** Good hook, then a sermon outline: David and Mother Teresa as facts, a four-item list of
   abstract nouns, an ending on a verse.
2. **Fixes.** Carry the chat joke through ("God was replying, just not in that chat"); give each receipt a "so
   what"; turn each list item into a moment; pay off the hook ("wrong inbox"); loop.
3. **Ken's notes that became rules.** Drop the exam-hall analogy; keep David and Mother Teresa; explain *how* God
   speaks (Word, people, a sermon, looking back at your life); new host with no beard; split in two; 40 seconds
   each; a spoken segue in Part 2 before "One"; no black CTA box; labels must fit their frames.
4. **Cut for time.** From Part 1: "So either God isn't real, or he's blocked my number. Before you decide, meet
   two people." From Part 2: "But here's what I missed… just not in that chat", "Look back at your life", the
   spoken Acts 17:27 line (kept as an on-screen card), "So go check again."

## Sources used

- Mother Teresa's decades of felt absence: *Come Be My Light* (2007), reported by Christian Century and America
  Magazine. The narration paraphrases; it does not quote her.
- Psalm 13:1; Acts 17:27; (in the long cut) 1 Kings 19:12.
